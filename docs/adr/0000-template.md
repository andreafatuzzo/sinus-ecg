# ADR NNNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (date of the last status change)
- **Deciders:** project owner (decision); tech lead (proposal)
- **Related:** requirement, hazard, risk-control and open-point IDs; documents and sections

## Context

What must be decided and why now:
- the forces at play: requirements, risks, constraints, the current state of the repository;
- the facts the decision depends on, with sources.

## Options considered

For each option: a short description, its advantages and its drawbacks. Include "do nothing" when it is a real option.

## Decision

The option chosen, stated so that it can be checked, plus any rules that follow from it (e.g. constraints on code, tools or interfaces).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what becomes easier or safer.
- **Negative and risks:** what becomes harder, what can go wrong, and how each risk is mitigated.
- **Follow-up:** documents to update, requirements or risk controls to add, open points (`OP-xxx`) created or closed.
